Create a modern, clean, high-converting B2B agricultural marketplace homepage built with Tailwind CSS. 

### PRODUCT OVERVIEW
- Platform Name: AgriBridge (Ethiopia's Direct B2B Wholesale Crop Marketplace)
- Core Business: Field agents in regional collection hubs (Bishoftu, Asella, Gondar, etc.) purchase crops directly from smallholder farmers, verify quality, and aggregate stock in hubs. 
- Homepage Target Audience: Consumer Buyers ,Wholesalers, grain processors, food manufacturers, and exporters looking to purchase produce in bulk

### DESIGN & VIBE
- Visual Style: Professional B2B SaaS / Fintech Marketplace aesthetic. High-contrast typography, clean data layout, subtle borders, and modern agricultural tones. Avoid using generic AI favoured coloring.
- Tone: Trustworthy, data-driven, premium, and efficient.

### REQUIRED HOMEPAGE SECTIONS

1. NAVIGATION BAR:
   - AgriBridge Logo with icon.
   - Links: "Browse Inventory", "Market Prices", "How Procurement Works".
   - Primary CTA: "Sign In / Register".
2. HERO SECTION (BUILT FOR  BUYERS):
   - Strong headline focused on wholesale buyers: e.g., "Source Verified Bulk Produce Directly From Ethiopian Regional Hubs."
   - Subheadline explaining guaranteed agent-checked quality, fixed transparent per-quintal rates, and automated logistics to their warehouse.
   - Add an appropriate background image(with link)
3. LIVE STOCK MARKETPLACE GRID (MAIN FEATURE):
   - Product Cards displaying real-time available inventory lots at collection hubs.
   - Each card must display:
     - Crop Name & Grade Tag (e.g., "White Teff - Grade 1 Magna")
     - Collection Hub Location (e.g., "Bishoftu Hub, Oromia")
     - Available Volume in Quintals (e.g., "450 Quintals ready")
     - Guaranteed Price per Quintal in ETB (e.g., "ETB 11,500 / Qtl")
     - Harvest Date & Agent Verification Badge
     - Primary Action Button: "Buy Lot / Place Order"

4. HOW IT WORKS FOR BUYERS (3-STEP PROCESS):
   - Step 1: Browse & Reserve verified produce stock from regional hubs.
   - Step 2: Lock in standardized company market prices with secure payment holds.
   - Step 3: Track integrated transport delivery directly to your facility.

5. FOOTER:
   - Platform information, marketplace quick links, documentation.
