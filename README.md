# AgriBridge

AgriBridge is a digital marketplace and coordination service for agricultural produce in Ethiopia. It connects produce recorded at local hubs with buyers who want to find and order available stock, while helping local agents, administrators, and transporters coordinate the work around each order.

## How it works

1. **Agents record produce at their hub.** They register farmers and add crop lots with a quantity and quality grade. Each crop uses a fixed price set for the marketplace.
2. **Buyers find and order stock.** Buyers browse available produce by crop, grade, and hub, choose a quantity, and select a destination hub. They can follow their orders and view their receipts from the buyer dashboard.
3. **Transporters move orders.** Approved transporters use an approved vehicle to take available buyer orders. They update the shipment as it is loaded, travels, and reaches its destination.
4. **Hubs can request replenishment.** When an agent's hub needs more stock, the agent can request produce from a nearby hub. AgriBridge checks nearby availability while keeping a reserve at the source, then creates trips sized for registered vehicles and assigns them to eligible transporters as they become available.
5. **Destination agents confirm hub deliveries.** Agents assigned to the receiving hub review incoming replenishment deliveries. If they report a problem, an administrator reviews it and can complete, retry, or cancel the transfer.

## Who uses AgriBridge

- **Buyers** search marketplace stock, place orders, track delivery, and review receipts. Buyer accounts require administrator verification before purchasing.
- **Agents** manage produce and farmer records for their assigned hub, request replenishment, and approve or report problems with incoming hub deliveries.
- **Transporters** register vehicles for inspection, carry assigned or available shipments, and update delivery progress.
- **Administrators** manage staff accounts and hub assignments, verify buyers, set crop prices, approve vehicle inspections, and resolve reported delivery problems.

## Current scope

AgriBridge supports produce listings, marketplace orders, shipment tracking, hub-to-hub replenishment, vehicle review, and delivery issue resolution. Payment processing is outside the current project scope; the fixed crop prices are used to show the order value, and the app does not collect or transfer payment.
