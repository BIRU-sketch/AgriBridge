How It Works

A simple and transparent process from supply to delivery.

  1. 1{{ icon('users') }}

    Register Farmers

    Extension workers or cooperative agents register farmers.

  2. 2{{ icon('leaf') }}

    List Produce

    Agents register available produce lots including crop, quantity, quality, price, location and availability.

  3. 3{{ icon('search') }}

    Match Supply & Demand

    The system evaluates produce against verified buyer demands using a transparent 0–100 matching score.

  4. 4{{ icon('truck') }}

    Coordinate Transport

    After the buyer and agent agree to a match, transportation is coordinated.

  5. 5{{ icon('check-circle') }}

    Confirm Delivery

    Shipment moves through: Assigned → Picked Up → In Transit → Delivered. The buyer confirms delivery.
