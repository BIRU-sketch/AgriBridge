How It Works

From searching available stock to receiving delivery at your destination — five steps, one platform.

  1. 1{{ icon('search') }}

    Search Inventory

    Browse available grain, pulse, and oilseed lots stored across regional aggregation hubs.

  2. 2{{ icon('leaf') }}

    Review Quality & Price

    Check quality grades, available tonnage, and fixed prices per quintal before committing.

  3. 3{{ icon('clipboard-check') }}

    Place Your Order

    Select a quantity and destination hub. Stock is reserved immediately — no double-selling.

  4. 4{{ icon('truck') }}

    Carrier Is Assigned

    A verified transporter is automatically dispatched with a digital consignment waybill.

  5. 5{{ icon('check-circle') }}

    Track & Receive

    Follow each delivery milestone — loading, transit, offloading — until settlement.
