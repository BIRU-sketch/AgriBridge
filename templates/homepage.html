Transparent Process

How AgriBridge Coordinates Trades

1

Publish Produce & Demand

Agents list available crops on behalf of farmers. Buyers submit their procurement requirements.

2

Transparent Match Engine

The engine calculates a transparent 0–100 score based on crop, quality, location, and price.

3

Mutual Agreement

Both Agent and Buyer must explicitly accept the deal. Trades are never automatically forced.

4

Shipment Tracking

Transporters execute pickup. Status updates live through Assigned → Picked Up → Delivered.
